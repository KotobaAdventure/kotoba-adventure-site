About Kotoba Adventure

Learn Japanese by falling in love with the world behind the words.

We believe the best way to learn a language is to fall in love with the world it belongs to.

Our mission with Kotoba Adventure is to convey the endless fascination and depth of the Japanese language through an engaging story set in Japan.

Language is more than just communication. It is a window into a culture's soul. By traveling through Japan with Luka and Philia, and learning the secrets of words from Kotoha-sensei, you will experience Japanese not just as a student, but as an adventurer.

We poured our love for Japan, anime, and the beauty of words into this project. We hope you enjoy the journey as much as we enjoyed creating it.

N5 Word Study Card

たべる

taberu

to eat

Example

あさごはんを たべます。

I eat breakfast.

Kotoha's Note

In casual speech, you'll often hear たべる. In polite speech, use たべます.
