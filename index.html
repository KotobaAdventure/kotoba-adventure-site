02 Daily Kotoba

One small word. One useful win.

Mini lessons for learners who want a tiny, readable piece of Japanese every day.

N5 Word Study Card

たべる

taberu

to eat

Example

あさごはんを たべます。

I eat breakfast.

Kotoha's Note

In casual speech, you'll often hear たべる. In polite speech, use たべます.
